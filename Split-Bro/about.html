ABOUT SPLIT BRO

Making shared expenses simpler.

Split Bro helps friends, roommates and groups manage shared expenses, understand who owes what, and settle payments easily.

Our Idea

Managing group expenses can become confusing when different people pay at different times. Split Bro was created to make expense tracking, splitting and settlement easier in one place.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a simple and easy-to-use platform where groups can manage shared expenses without complicated calculations.
